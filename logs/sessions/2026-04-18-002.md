## Decisions Made
- Axis labels removed from Vico chart (no startAxis/bottomAxis) — replaced by MIN/MAX/AVG/CHANGE stat cards below; cleaner look without decimal noise
- `WFTokens` design tokens used directly in screens instead of relying on Material3 semantic tokens
- `NavigationBar` gets `tonalElevation = 0.dp` + explicit `containerColor` — prevents Material3 tonal lift darkening the bg
