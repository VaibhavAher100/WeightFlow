## Reasoning

Keystore decode-in-CI pattern is standard for Android Play Store builds — base64 avoids binary secret handling, step-scoped env vars prevent credential leakage to other steps, always-run cleanup ensures the `/tmp` keystore is removed even on build failure. Opus 4.7 validated the approach before execution.

R8 ProGuard `-dontwarn` for Tink annotations is the Google-recommended fix (Tink's own migration guide acknowledges this). The annotations (`@CanIgnoreReturnValue`, `@Immutable`, etc.) are compile-time only and have no runtime impact.
