## Reasoning
Codex adversarial review is valuable as a challenge pass. In this case both findings were arithmetically incorrect:
1. The gain-goal formula uses `(start - current) / (start - goal)`. When goal > start, the denominator is negative. A regression below start makes the numerator positive, yielding a negative quotient, clamped to 0. Correct.
2. All WeightFlow entries are stored at `atStartOfDay()`. A 6-days-ago entry is at most `6*24h + 23h59m = 167.999h` old — always within the 168h cutoff. No calendar drift possible with this storage model.

Adding the tests anyway was the right call — they now document the expected behavior and will catch any future refactor that breaks gain-goal math or the avg window.
