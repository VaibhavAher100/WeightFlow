## Decisions Made

- **Edit entry is P0 not P1** — Opus correctly identified fat-finger with no edit = instant 1-star reviews
- **Age gate 13+ globally** — Opus: 18+ amputates most engaged demographic (fitness-focused 16-25)
- **No AdMob in v1.0** — Opus: 3 revenue paths = death for solo dev. One-time Pro only
- **Reminder opt-in (not auto)** — Codex: unconditional WorkManager scheduling without user consent is bad
- **Release signing fail-fast** — Codex: silent debug fallback means signing failures go undetected
- **Age gate flash fix** — Codex: `initialValue=true` briefly shows shell to new users; tri-state fixes
- **GitHub Pages from docs/ on main** — simplest; Jekyll renders markdown; avoids separate gh-pages branch
- **Repo public** — needed for Pages on free plan; Play Store discovery benefits anyway
- **W mark as icon** — W shape reads as a weight-trend chart line; data-point circle at peak; distinctive
- **open-design craft rules applied** — Display text negative tracking, ALL-CAPS positive tracking, body = 0
