## Keyword Strategy

| Tier | Keywords | Where to place |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Primary** | weight tracker, weight loss tracker | Title + first line of full desc |
| **Secondary** | BMI calculator, weight log, weigh-in diary, scale tracker | Short desc + feature bullets |
| **Long-tail** | free weight tracker no subscription, offline weight tracker, private weight log, switch from WeightFit | Closing keyword paragraph + reviews |
| **Differentiators** | offline, private, no ads, no subscription, CSV import, GDPR | Throughout — these are the moat |

**Play-specific:** the full description is indexed, so long-tail phrases and synonyms
earn ranking. Google understands semantic similarity — vary phrasing, never stuff.
User reviews are also indexed: encourage reviewers to mention "free", "no ads",
"switched from WeightFit".

## Ratings Strategy (Play ratings never reset)

- Wire the Play In-App Review API (`ReviewManager.requestReviewFlow()`) to fire after a
  success moment — e.g. hitting a goal or a 7-day logging streak, never on cold open.
- Respond to every 1–3★ review; replies invite re-rating and are algorithmically positive.
- When a complaint is fixed, reply "Fixed in vX.X" — recovers the rating.
