## 2. What Data We Collect and Why

WeightFlow is a **privacy-first, offline app**. All data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers, and your data does not leave your device.

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|------|---------|---------|
| Weight entries (value, date, unit) | Core tracking | On-device (Room DB) |
| Profile (name, height, goal weight, year of birth) | Goal tracking, BMI | On-device (Room DB) |
| Preferences (theme, unit, notifications) | Customisation | On-device (DataStore) |

We do **not** collect: email addresses, location data, device identifiers, advertising IDs, or third-party health data.

## 3. Legal Basis (GDPR)

- **Art. 6(1)(b) — Performance of contract:** Weight and profile data required to deliver the service.
- **Art. 6(1)(a) — Consent:** Notification reminders, based on your explicit opt-in. Withdraw any time in Settings.

---

## 4. Data Retention

Data is stored on your device until you delete it via **Profile → Delete all data** or uninstall the app. No data is retained on external servers.

---

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR)

- **Access (Art. 15):** All data is visible in-app.
- **Portability (Art. 20):** Export CSV via Settings → Export data.
- **Erasure (Art. 17):** Delete all data via Profile → Delete all data.
- **Rectification (Art. 16):** Edit/delete individual entries in History.
- **Withdraw consent:** Disable notifications in Settings.

Contact vaibhavaher100@gmail.com for any other requests. We respond within 30 days.

---

## 6. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

**Google Play Store** distributes WeightFlow. Google may collect device/installation metadata per [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

WeightFlow is intended for users aged **13 and over** (COPPA minimum). The app enforces a year-of-birth gate — users under 13 cannot complete onboarding.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Contact vaibhavaher100@gmail.com to request deletion if needed.

---

## 8. Security

Data is stored in Android's private app sandbox. Release builds block unencrypted (HTTP) traffic. Device-to-device transfer and cloud backup are both disabled — use CSV export to migrate data.
